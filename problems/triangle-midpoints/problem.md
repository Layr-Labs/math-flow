# Triangle midpoint quadrilateral

Let points \(D\), \(E\), and \(F\) be the midpoints of sides \(BC\), \(CA\), and
\(AB\) of a nondegenerate triangle \(ABC\). Prove that the four small triangles
\(AEF\), \(BFD\), \(CDE\), and \(DEF\) have equal area.

Contributions may give synthetic, coordinate, formal, or computational arguments,
or may critique and extend earlier contributions.
